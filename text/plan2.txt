Survey of what's actually missing vs. c/libxaac: the AAC-LC/HE-AAC v1/v2 (SBR+PS) path is close to parity — I checked, and the
  C encoder itself doesn't implement PNS, intensity stereo, or pulse coding either, so those aren't real gaps. The genuine, large
  gaps are all in the USAC/xHE-AAC layer, and they're bigger than they look:
  - src/decoder/usac and src/encoder/usac are non-functional placeholders today, not real implementations (the ACELP decoder
    ignores its bitstream reader; the TCX decoder just linearly scales integers instead of running an inverse transform), despite
    their doc comments claiming otherwise.
  - Real USAC needs: a context-adaptive arithmetic coder for spectral data (the foundation everything else sits on), ACELP+TCX
    speech coding, MPEG Surround multichannel encoding, complex-prediction stereo, and enhanced-SBR extensions. 
  - Separately, DRC is also smaller in Rust than in C: what's implemented is the older, simple dynamic_range_info() element,
    while the C codebase has a full ~8,000-line MPEG-D Unified DRC encoder (gain calculator, multiband EQ, config muxing).

  I've dispatched a background agent to port the USAC spectral arithmetic coder first, since it's the foundational piece
  everything else in USAC depends on — real algorithm and tables from ixheaacd_arith_dec.c/iusace_arith_enc.c, not a simplified
  stand-in, with round-trip tests. I'll review its diff carefully and integrate it once it lands, then keep working down the list
  (ACELP/TCX, MPS encoder, or uniDrc — whichever makes sense to sequence next). I'll report back when that's done.
